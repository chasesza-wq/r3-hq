Questions

The ones we get every week.

Will pressure damage my roof or siding?

Not the way we do it. Roofs and painted siding get a soft wash — low pressure plus the correct cleaning solution, which kills the organic growth rather than blasting it off along with your paint. High pressure is only used on concrete and paver surfaces.

Do you need my water and power?

No. The rig carries its own tank and generator, so nothing shows up on your utility bill and we can work an empty property.

How long until the green comes back?

In Florida, untreated concrete starts to re-shade in about four to six months. Every job here finishes with a post-treatment that roughly doubles that. Twice-a-year customers never see it return.

What about my plants?

Beds get soaked before and rinsed after, which dilutes anything that reaches them. We have never replaced a customer's landscaping and we'd rather keep it that way.

Do I need to be home?

Only if the gate is locked. Most exterior jobs are quoted from a photo, done while you're at work, and paid by link once you have walked it.
