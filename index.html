Before you ask

The questions everyone asks on the call.

What if it doesn’t make me money?
You will see it in the portal — we report conservatively-attributed dollars, not vanity clicks. After the first 90 days you are month to month; if the numbers do not clear your cost, fire us. That risk is ours on purpose.
Does the AI actually sound human?
Yes — and you will hear it yourself: ask on your audit call and we will ring it together. It knows your services and prices, asks the questions your best front-desk person asks, and hands complicated calls to you with a text summary.
Seven business days — really?
Really. Day 1 is the kickoff call; by day 7 you approve it on a screen share and we flip it live. We can move that fast because we only build for local service businesses, on a system we have refined build after build.
Do I own the website?
Yes — stay 12 months on any plan and the site is 100% yours: code, domain, content. Leave before 12 months and there is a one-time $997 buy-out if you want to take it with you. Either way, no hostage hosting — nothing breaks if we part ways.
What does the setup fee cover?
The actual build — your website designed and launched, your Google profile rebuilt, your tracking number wired in, and on the bigger plans your AI receptionist trained on your services and prices. You pay for that work once. The monthly covers everything after: hosting, maintenance, monitoring, edits and reporting. No surprise invoices, no hourly billing.
What if it doesn’t fit neatly — I run a gym, salon or med spa?
Same plans, same prices — the build is what changes. Repeat-visit businesses get booking flows, reminders and no-show recovery wired in; job-call businesses get dispatch-style call handling and reactivation campaigns. Tell us what you run on the audit call and we configure your plan around it.
