Questions

What people ask before they start.

Does this replace my front desk?

No. It catches the calls your front desk can't get to: during check-in, at lunch, after close. They still take every call they can.

What number does the text come from?

A local business number registered for your office, set up behind the line you already publish. Patients keep calling the number they know.

What goes in the texts?

Scheduling only: who you are, what they need, and open times. No medical details go over text.

What if someone doesn't want texts?

Every thread honors STOP. One reply and they're off the list for good.

What does it cost?

One setup fee, then a monthly that doesn't start until it's booked you 15 new appointments. We'll go through the exact numbers on the call.

How fast can it be live?

About a week from the intake form. You'll test it on your own phone before it goes live.
