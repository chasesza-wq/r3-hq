How to opt in

You join this program by submitting the audit request form at recoverrevenue.co/book.html with your mobile number and checking the consent box, which is unchecked by default. The box reads:

“I agree to receive text messages from RecoverRevenue at the mobile number provided, including messages about my audit, qualifying questions, and appointment scheduling and reminders. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.”

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of receiving your audit or of purchasing any product or service from us. If you do not check the box, we will still deliver your audit by email.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any message from us at any time. You will receive one final message confirming that you have been unsubscribed, and we will send you no further messages in this program. You may also email chase@recoverrevenue.co to be removed. To rejoin, submit the form again or reply START.
