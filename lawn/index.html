Questions

The ones we get every week.

What happens when it rains?

We push to the next dry day in the same week, not the next fortnight. If a whole week is lost, it is made up — you are never billed for a cut that did not happen.

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers are at work. We text before we arrive, latch the gate on the way out, and send a photo of the finished lawn.

Why flat monthly instead of per cut?

Because Florida grass grows four times faster in July than in January, and per-cut billing means your summer invoices double exactly when you notice them most. The flat rate is the year averaged out.

Can you take over mid-season?

Yes, and the first visit is usually a longer one to bring the height back down gradually. We never scalp a lawn to catch it up — that is how you get a brown yard in August.

Do you handle the beds and hedges too?

Bed edges and light hedge work are in the weekly plan. Bigger landscaping, mulch and palm trimming are quoted separately so you are not paying for them all year.

Pick a day. We'll keep it.

Tell us the address and we'll quote from the lot size — usually within the hour, and the number does not change once you're on the route.
